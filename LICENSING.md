## Artwork and image assets

The original mascot sprites, illustrations, and covered promotional artwork are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International** (**CC BY-NC 4.0**).

You may copy, share, and modify those assets for non-commercial purposes, provided that you give appropriate credit, link to the license, and indicate whether changes were made.

Commercial use of the covered artwork is not authorized by the CC BY-NC 4.0 license. A separate commercial license must be obtained from the copyright holder.

See `ASSETS-LICENSE.md` for the exact scope and attribution instructions. The complete CC BY-NC 4.0 legal code is included in `LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-4.0.txt`.
